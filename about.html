ABOUT US

WMPKD is a student-owned handmade snacks brand right here at UNIBEN. We started as a small passion project and have grown into one of the most loved crunchy snack brands in Benin City.

Every pack is freshly made daily with quality local ingredients and lots of love. We believe good snacks should be crunchy, tasty, affordable, and delivered fast to students.
