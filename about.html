About WMPKD

Our Story of Quality and Passion

Our Story

WMPKD started as a passion project by a student at the University of Benin (UNIBEN). What began as making snacks for friends and family has grown into a thriving business dedicated to delivering premium handmade snacks.

Every product is crafted with care, using only the finest ingredients. We believe that quality snacks should be accessible to everyone, and we're committed to maintaining the highest standards in everything we do.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, delicious handmade snacks that bring joy to our customers while supporting local entrepreneurship and sustainable practices.

Our Vision

To become the go-to snack brand for students and professionals, known for quality, reliability, and exceptional customer service.
